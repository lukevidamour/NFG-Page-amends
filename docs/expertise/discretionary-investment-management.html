Our Core Strategies.

Balanced

Our dynamic take on a classic medium-risk investment strategy. We actively manage our balanced mandate to seek shelter in challenging times and pursue a pro-growth bias when opportunities are plentiful. We have the freedom to exclude major asset classes from this investment mandate, thereby deviating materially from our peers and industry benchmarks to deliver superior returns while managing risk more effectively.

Multi-Asset Growth

We take a bold approach to this growth strategy, which is available as a portfolio, or via the NFG Partners SPC - Multi-Asset SP Fund. We have broad ranges for the investment in equities, fixed income, commodities, and specialist funds to deliver growth-biased returns in a higher-risk investment strategy. We enjoy particularly wide investment latitudes, which maximises the opportunity set available to us.

Global Equity

This predominantly equity-based strategy is for high-risk investors seeking the long-term benefits of investing in equity markets. We actively manage underlying allocations; however, this strategy will consistently be 80% - 100% allocated to stock market instruments. Our equity mandate has no persistent style bias. We will invest in value, growth, low volatility, REITs, Emerging markets, and commodity stocks globally as our evidence-based investment approach dictates.
