About Us.

NFG Partners is the Wealth and Investment division of NFG SA, a diversified family owned conglomerate active in the financial services sector and headquartered in London. 

We provide exceptional financial guidance, empowering clients with innovative tailored solutions to achieve their unique needs.

Foresight

Our proactive approach helps navigate market changes and seize new opportunities, ensuring long-term growth and security.

Integrity

We prioritize honesty and transparency, ensuring your interests come first.

Intelligence

Using a time-tested investment process and deep industry knowledge, we deliver data-driven strategies for financial success.

Our Team.

Meet our experienced professionals, dedicated to excellence:
Zam Manji

Managing Director

Zam has had over 15 years experience in the financial service sector – both as a private & investment banker. He started his career in the Fixed Income division of Bear Stearns and JP Morgan in London, before shifting into Private Banking with Royal Bank of Canada Suisse and Banque SYZ following their acquisition in 2015 in Dubai and Geneva. A few years later he moved to Banque J. Safra Sarasin in Geneva. Zam has built strong relations with his clients and partners, with extensive knowledge on Swiss and Emerging markets. He is a graduate of Warwick Business School.

Yohan Palleau

Director

Recognized by Citywire as one of Switzerland’s Top Talent under 30, Yohan Palleau is Co-Founder and Director of NFG Partners SA. As part of the executive team, he oversees client relationships with a focus on global entrepreneurs, families and institutions seeking independent, cross-border wealth management. Yohan began his career within the BPCE Group before joining the Deal Team at ACE & Company, a Swiss private equity firm. He later moved to Banque SYZ, where he advised private and institutional clients across the UK, Middle East, and Africa developing tailored investment strategies and long-term structuring solutions. He holds a Bachelor of Science in Management from HEC Lausanne and a Master of Science in Wealth Management from the University of Geneva.

Ryan Corton

Chief Operating Officer

Ryan brings over 20 years of financial services expertise, focused on operational infrastructure, compliance and investment strategy. Before joining NFG Partners SA, he served as Chief Operating Officer & Chief Compliance Officer at Chrystal Capital Partners, a corporate advisory firm specialising in capital raising and structuring. He previously held the roles of Group COO and Chief Compliance Officer at Harwood Capital Management, a leading private wealth, investment and private equity manager and was Operations Director at J O Hambro Capital Management. Ryan has led fund launches in the Cayman Islands, Guernsey, Ireland, and London and has held board positions at several regulated investment firms. He has deep experience in operations, compliance, joint ventures, digital transformation, and process optimisation. Ryan is a regulated and approved individual in Switzerland with the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority, in the UK by the Financial Conduct Authority, the Central Bank of Ireland, and the Guernsey Financial Services Commission and has provided operational advisory to numerous investment companies globally.

Paul Sarosy

Non-Executive Director

Paul brings over 35 years of wealth management experience to NFG Partners, having held senior leadership positions at Merrill Lynch International, Credit Suisse, Coutts and a Multi-Family Office. Throughout his career, Paul has led teams across discretionary and advisory investment management, product development and marketing. A strong focus on understanding and serving clients' needs, combined with open, transparent and consistent communication, has been a hallmark of his work in the industry. Based in London, Paul has extensive experience working with clients and families across the UK, Europe and the Middle East. Paul continues to advise families and also works as a consultant and coach within the wealth management industry. As a Non-Executive Director of NFG Partners, he brings extensive client experience and industry insight to the Board.

Glenn Coxon

Chief Investment Officer

Glenn began his investment management career in 1997 and has managed private client and family office wealth ever since. He was a member of the Asset Allocation, Fund Selection and Alternatives committees with Collins Stewart before moving to Switzerland in 2008. Glenn founded Harver Capital in 2015, a boutique asset manager, delivering a strong track record with a unique Evidence Based investment process for multi-asset portfolios. In 2023 the Harver Capital team joined NFG Partners to further enhance their asset management capabilities. Glenn is lead manager of the NFG Partners SPC – NFG Multi-Asset fund.

Joshua Oldham, CFA, CMT

Investment Manager

Josh joined Harver Capital in 2016 after completing a Masters in Mathematical Finance at York. Josh manages client portfolios, undertakes investment research and modelling as well as providing analytical support to the business. Josh has eight years of hands on asset management experience and joined NFG Partners in 2023. He is both a CFA and CMT (Chartered Market Technician) charter holder.

Nicholas Bedford, CAIA

Investment Manager

A passionate investment professional, Nick joined NFG Partners at the beginning of 2024 building out and overseeing our Advisory offering. Previously, he has worked in Geneva, London and Zurich – starting out at Moneycorp in London, then a Swiss based independent asset manager, followed by 5 and a half years at Barclays private Bank - taking care of UK Residents non-Domiciles. Nick then joined Mirabaud Private Bank where he ran the Advisory desk for the UK & International team for four and a half years, before joining NFG Partners. Nick is also a CAIA charterholder and an associate member of the CISI (Chartered Institute for Securities & Investment).

Alessandro D’Errico

Relationship Manager

Alessandro brings strong expertise in capital markets, credit and private equity, honed at innovative FinTechs as well as established Swiss institutions. He began his career at Nanteos Capital in Zug, advising private and corporate clients on bespoke financial solutions. In Zurich, he led a team at Acredius, structuring credit and private equity transactions for startups and SMEs. Most recently at Saxo Bank in Zurich, Alessandro managed a multi-asset portfolio for high-net-worth and corporate clients across Switzerland, Europe and the Middle East. He holds an MBA from the SP Jain School of Global Management.

Kelly Mourao

Executive Assistant & Operations

Kelly has over 20 years of experience in executive support, office management, and operations within international financial institutions, private banking, asset management, and commodities companies. Before joining NFG Partners, she held Executive Assistant and Office Management roles at Banque Pictet, Société Générale Private Banking (Suisse), Lynx Energy Trading, and Trafigura. Supporting senior executives and leadership teams in highly confidential and fast-paced environments, she has developed extensive expertise in executive coordination, international operations, event management, and stakeholder engagement. As part of the NFG Partners team, Kelly plays a key role in supporting the firm's operations and ensuring the smooth delivery of services to both clients and colleagues. She is recognized for her professionalism, discretion, and strong organizational skills.
