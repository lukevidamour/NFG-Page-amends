NFG Partners appoints Paul Sarosy as Non-Executive Director

Geneva, Switzerland – 01.09.2026


NFG Partners is pleased to announce the appointment of Paul Sarosy as a Non-Executive Director and member of its Board.


Paul brings more than 35 years of wealth management experience, having held senior leadership positions at Merrill Lynch International, Credit Suisse, Coutts and a Multi-Family Office. Throughout his career, he has led teams across discretionary and advisory investment management, product development and marketing. His appointment follows a period of close collaboration with NFG Partners, during which Paul has acted as a consultant to the Board.


Paul's extensive experience and strong understanding of the needs of private clients and families will further strengthen the Board as NFG Partners continues to develop its business.


Speaking about the appointment, Zam Manji, Founder of NFG Partners, said:

“A real coup for us at NFG Partners SA, bringing someone of Paul’s experience and standing in the industry will add enormous value to both our clients and the firm. It’s also a great endorsement of the work we've done and what we’re building here that Paul has chosen to join us.”


Paul Sarosy added:

“I am thrilled to be working as a NED with the NFG Partners team. Their stellar performance track record (10+years) combined with their genuine personal client engagement is rare and refreshing to see."


Based in London, Paul has extensive experience serving clients and families across the UK, Europe and the Middle East. He continues to advise families and works as a consultant and coach within the wealth management industry.
